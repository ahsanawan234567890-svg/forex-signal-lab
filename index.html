Data source, privacy & limitations
  • Live data is fetched from Twelve Data's documented time-series endpoint, if your plan/key permits it.
  • This demo supports candle intervals of 1 minute and above. It does not manufacture 5-second/10-second candles from 1-minute candles.
  • Regular Forex data is not platform-specific OTC data. OTC mode is deliberately not offered without an authorized matching feed.
  • The analysis is deterministic technical analysis, not a trained AI model. An AI API would require a separate model/service and secure server-side integration.
  • For a real accuracy claim, use chronological out-of-sample backtests with spread, slippage, fees, and enough trades. A few successful signals are not proof.
  • Use for research only. Not financial advice. No orders are executed.
